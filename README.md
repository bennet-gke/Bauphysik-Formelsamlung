## <a id="deutsch"></a>🇩🇪 Deutsch

### 📖 Zweck dieser Formelsammlung

Diese Formelsammlung wurde als **klausurbegleitendes Nachschlagewerk** für die Bauphysik-Module an der **HafenCity Universität Hamburg (HCU)** erstellt. Sie fasst alle relevanten Formeln, Herleitungen und Zusammenhänge aus der Vorlesung von **Prof. Dr.-Ing. Ingo Weidlich** und **Christian Steuck** zusammen.

Die Bauphysik beschäftigt sich im Kern mit den **Energieflüssen im und durch Gebäude** – sie ist die Grundlage, um Baukonstruktion und Gebäudetechnik richtig zu verstehen. Diese Sammlung deckt die drei großen Säulen des Fachs ab: **Wärme**, **Feuchte** und **Schall**.

---

## <a id="englisch"></a>🇬🇧 English

### 📖 Purpose of this Formula Collection

This formula collection was created as a **reference guide for exams** in the Building Physics modules at **HafenCity Universität Hamburg (HCU)**. It summarizes all relevant formulas, derivations, and correlations from the lectures held by **Prof. Dr.-Ing. Ingo Weidlich** and **Christian Steuck**.

At its core, building physics deals with the **energy flows in and through buildings** – it is the foundation for understanding building construction and building services engineering. This collection covers the three major pillars of the subject: **Heat**, **Moisture**, and **Sound**.

---

### 📂 Topic Overview

The formula collection is structured according to the lecture weeks and contains the following content:

#### Part A: Heat Transfer & Building Component Physics

| Chapter | Topic | Core Content |
|---------|-------|--------------|
| **1** | Steady-State Heat Transfer | Heat conduction (λ), U-Value, multi-layer components, adjacent components, thermal bridges |
| **2** | Thermal Radiation | Planck's law, Wien's displacement law, radiative exchange between plane-parallel surfaces |
| **3** | Summer Thermal Protection | Solar gain, shading, heating up of rooms |
| **4** | Transient Heat Transfer | Temporal heating/cooling of components, penetration depth, phase shift |
| **5** | Solar Thermal Energy | Collector types, efficiency, solar domestic hot water heating |

#### Part B: Moisture Protection

| Chapter | Topic | Core Content |
|---------|-------|--------------|
| **6** | Air Humidity & Dew Point | Absolute/Relative humidity, water vapor partial pressure, dew point temperature, moisture equilibrium |
| **7** | Glaser Method | Water vapor diffusion, diffusion resistance (µ, sd-value), Glaser diagram, condensation assessment |

#### Part C: Acoustics (Sound Theory)

| Chapter | Topic | Core Content |
|---------|-------|--------------|
| **8** | Vibrations & Waves | Frequency, wavelength, speed of sound |
| **9** | Sound Field Quantities & Levels | Sound pressure level (dB), sound power level, A-/C-weighting, isophonic curves |
| **10** | Sound Propagation | Distance laws, reflection, absorption, diffraction, refraction |
| **11** | Room Acoustics & Reverberation | Sabine formula, reverberation time, diffuse sound field, absorption coefficients |
| **12** | Airborne Sound Insulation | Mass law, single-leaf/double-leaf walls, coincidence effect |
| **13** | Flanking Transmission & Impact Sound | Structure-borne sound, flanking components, impact sound insulation |

#### Part D: Systems & Energy

| Chapter | Topic | Core Content |
|---------|-------|--------------|
| **14** | Photovoltaics | Solar cells, efficiency, characteristic curves |
| **15** | Heat Pumps | Thermodynamic cycle, COP, seasonal performance factor |
